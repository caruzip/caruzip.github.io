BC Time Zone Change Planning: What It Means For Your Calendar and Devices

Published: October 22 2026   |   Campus Operations

This notice outlines the standard steps for reviewing schedules and systems when a time zone change takes effect.

Update - British Columbia has adopted permanent year-round daylight saving time (Pacific Time), with the change scheduled to take effect on November 1. We recognize that some community members may have questions about the impact on calendars, devices, and scheduled meetings. The University of Westend is actively working with Microsoft and Apple to coordinate the required time zone updates for computers, mobile devices, and calendaring services. We anticipate updates from our vendors will begin rolling out next week.

Step 1: Install updates and restart your computer.

Managed Windows computers: Restart when prompted. The update is installed automatically.

Managed Macs: Restart when prompted, especially if you have not restarted in a while.

Step 2: Check your time zone settings.

Confirm that your device and calendar applications use the appropriate time zone and update automatically where supported.

Step 3. Confirm shared schedules. Update the meetings you organized.

Update any meeting you organized that takes place on or after November 1, including recurring series. Open the meeting (or the whole series), turn on or select the time zone option, choose (UTC-07:00) Pacific Time (British Columbia), and send the update.

Step 4. November 1 onward. Double-check.

Confirm the start times of important meetings, especially those with people outside BC. If a time looks wrong, confirm it with the organizer.

Questions? Please contact the service desk.
